Good questions.

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need a website?

SiteMate needs a website because that’s where it answers questions and collects enquiries. JobDesk can be used with enquiries from any source. If you don’t have a website yet, Amplify can help you explore your options.

Can JobDesk handle enquiries from other sources?

Yes. You can manually add enquiries from phone calls, WhatsApp, social media and referrals, then keep your jobs, quotes and invoices together. A website isn’t required.

When is Echo coming?

Echo is planned for early 2027. It will answer business calls in an AI version of your own voice, with your permission, and capture job details. You can register interest through Amplify now.

Do the tools automatically pass jobs between them?

This example shows you adding a captured enquiry to JobDesk yourself. We’re not claiming an automatic handoff. Each tool helps with a different part of the working day.
