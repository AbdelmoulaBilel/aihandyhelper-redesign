Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Draft, pending final business details.

Who we are

Ai Handy Helper is a brand created and developed by AR Web Studios for UK tradespeople. This website helps you explore SiteMate, JobDesk, Echo and Relay. Registered business details and a contact address will be added here once confirmed.

What we collect

The tool finder asks about your trade, website, business needs and enquiry sources. It does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your answers remain in this page’s temporary state and are cleared when you reload or leave it.

How we use it

Your answers are used on this page to recommend tools. We do not send your tool-finder answers to Amplify. If you follow an external link and submit an enquiry, the destination website’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time, and you can withdraw marketing consent whenever you like. For enquiries submitted on Amplify, consult Amplify’s privacy policy and contact details. A contact email for this website’s privacy requests will be published here once confirmed.

Data storage and security

The tool finder does not save your answers to a database. The site keeps one small flag in your browser’s session storage so the power-on intro plays only once per visit. It holds no personal data and clears when you close the tab. Enquiry forms and early-access registration are handled on Amplify, not on this page.
